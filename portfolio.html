Email Marketing · Personalisation

Bespoke HTML Email Programmes Case Study

Situation
Banks, insurers and telecommunications providers needed recurring customer emails that stayed on-brand in every inbox — including Outlook — while carrying each recipient’s own details.
Task
Design and hand-code personalised HTML email templates that marketing and adviser teams could re-use across campaigns and customer segments.

Mobile · Billing Communication

Personalised MMS Billing Case Study

Situation
A telecommunications provider delivers monthly statements straight to customers’ handsets as multi-frame MMS messages, across several product lines.
Task
Design statement MMS sequences for fixed-line, mobile and next-generation network customers, each populated from billing data.
Action
Created 600 × 450 frame sequences — greeting, account information, summary, tax invoice and a closing campaign frame — built once as templates and prepared as storyboards for client approval before production.
Result
Three approved product variants sharing one structure, each with its own creative treatment, ready to be populated per customer every billing cycle.

Creative Mentorship · Community Engagement

Traditional Arts & Crafts Mentorship Case Study

Situation
High school youth in the Hartbeespoort community required expert guidance to master a wide array of traditional crafts to achieve national accreditation and earn their proficiency badges.
Task
Facilitate an immersive, hands-on mentorship programme for the local Kommando GGN, focusing on technical mastery across diverse physical mediums.
Action
Led intensive workshops in candle and soap making, fabric painting, and structural work with cement, wire, and paper. I simplified complex artisanal techniques—from wool-work to cable manipulation—into actionable milestones for the youth groups. I also designed the kommando’s supporting material — badge registration sheets, packing lists, session programmes, cap artwork and event e-cards.
Result
Empowered the next generation of makers with practical, lifelong creative skills and successfully guided all participants through the completion of their national badge requirements.
