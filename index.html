Featured Projects

Three featured projects. Real outcomes.

Personalised Video · Data-Driven Design

More than 10,000 personalised videos for one campaign

Situation
A global telecommunications company wanted to reach customers on WhatsApp with video that spoke to each person individually, not a single generic message.
Task
Design and produce thousands of unique videos, each driven by the customer's own data, without losing quality or consistency along the way.
Action
I built a data-driven creative framework: one master design with personalised fields, produced at volume with multimedia developers, voice artists and copywriters. I also design personalised onboarding and explainer videos for banking and insurance clients in South Africa and Australia.
Result
More than 10,000 unique personalised videos delivered for a single campaign, all built from one master design.
HTML Email · MMS · Statements

Billing communications that go out every month

Situation
Telecoms, banking and insurance brands send statements, invoices and service emails to huge customer bases every month. Every version has to look right, in every inbox and on every handset.
Task
Design communications that carry each customer's own details and figures, and stay clear and on-brand whatever the data, including in Outlook.
Action
I built a responsive HTML email template system used across all of iLiveIT's clients, designed Telkom's monthly invoice mailers for seven customer segments and its statement MMS for three product lines, and designed invoice layouts for MTN and, in Portuguese, Vodacom Moçambique.
Result
Turnaround for large clients fell from 4–5 days to under two, with up to 40 designs in production at once, each tested across popular email clients. The templates are re-used and re-sent every billing cycle.
Brand Identity · Workflow Automation

One brand across two continents: iLiveIT

Situation
iLiveIT worked across South Africa and Australia without one visual identity, and manual design workflows were slowing down delivery for its retail, banking and telecoms clients.
Task
Create a single international brand identity, and restructure the creative team's workflow so it could produce more, faster, without lowering standards.
Action
I designed the identity and its guidelines (colour, typography and responsive grids), introduced automation and new workflows in the studio, and recruited and mentored the creative team.
Result
One consistent brand in both markets, shorter project turnaround, and lower staff turnover through tailored development.

Work shown here was produced in-house at iLiveIT. The brands are not clients of Fervent Digital Marketing, and all trade marks remain the property of their owners.
